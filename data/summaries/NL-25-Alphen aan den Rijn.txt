Occupancy declined sharply in July, dropping from 86.9% to 84.4% on a unit basis and from 88.0% to 83.5% by square meter, driven by a net loss of 13 units as vacates (26) significantly outpaced move-ins (13). Rental velocity has deteriorated over the three-month window, with net rentals declining from +4 units in May to -13 units in July. Arrears have grown concerning, rising from 3,880 to 6,405 outstanding across 24 accounts (up from 20), while revenue has remained roughly flat despite the occupancy headwind, supported partly by a modest rate increase to 22.45 per sqm in July.
