Occupancy at 1BOX Alphen aan den Rijn declined sharply in July, falling 2.5 percentage points by unit count and 4.5 points by square meter, driven by a spike in vacates to 26 units against only 13 move-ins and resulting in a net loss of 13 units for the month. Recovery began in August with positive net rentals of 3 units as move-ins improved to 23, stabilizing occupancy at 85.0% by unit count and 84.0% by square meter. Revenue remained relatively stable across the three months (ranging from €59,119 to €60,557), and the average rate per square meter increased modestly from €21.85 to €22.62. Arrears spiked in July to €6,405 across 24 accounts but declined to €4,947 in August, though the account count rose to 30, suggesting payment behavior improved despite wider spread of minor outstanding balances.
