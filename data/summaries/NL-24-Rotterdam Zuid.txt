Occupancy has declined steadily over the three-month period, falling from 85.3% to 80.9% by unit count and from 88.6% to 85.8% by square metre, driven by accelerating net losses—the site shed 8 units in May, 10 in June, and 18 in July. Rental velocity has weakened significantly, with move-ins dropping from 14 to 12 while vacates increased from 21 to 29 month-over-month. Despite the occupancy pressure, revenue has grown from €84,191 to €90,402 and average rate per square metre has climbed from €23.41 to €26.00, indicating successful rate management offsetting volume losses. Arrears count has spiked to 139 units in July (up from 108 in May), though total outstanding amount remains relatively flat at €27,565, warranting closer collection attention.
