Occupancy at 1BOX Rotterdam Zuid declined from 83.9% to 80.8% over the three-month window, with the steepest drop occurring between June and July, followed by stabilization in August. Rental velocity deteriorated sharply in July with net negative 18 units, though the site showed recovery in August with only net negative 2 units as move-ins increased to 21. Revenue remained relatively stable in the 85k–90k range despite occupancy pressure, supported by rate-per-sqm increases from 24.32 to 26.07 across the period. Arrears remain elevated at 108 accounts and €20.7k outstanding as of August, though this represents an improvement from the July peak of 139 accounts and €27.6k.
