Occupancy remained flat across the three months, holding at approximately 80.8–80.9% by unit count and 85.1–85.8% by square meter, with minimal net rental activity (declining 18 units in July, then stabilizing near zero in August and September). Rental velocity improved modestly from a net loss of 18 units in July to breakeven in September, driven by higher move-ins in recent months (28 in September versus 12 in July) even as vacates remained elevated. Revenue declined slightly from €90,402 in July to €89,597 in September, while average rate per square meter slipped from €26.07 to €25.49. Arrears improved significantly, dropping from €27,565 across 139 accounts in July to €24,089 across 140 accounts by September, indicating better collection performance despite the flat occupancy trajectory.
