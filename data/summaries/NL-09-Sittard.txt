1BOX Sittard recovered from a weak July, adding net 6 units across August and September to reach 195 occupied units (64.1% unit occupancy) by September. Revenue grew steadily from €31,019 to €33,083 month-over-month, driven by both occupancy gains and rising rental rates (from €16.85 to €18.46 per sqm). Square-meter occupancy fluctuated slightly, ending at 69.7% in September, while arrears remained elevated at €5,361 outstanding across 33 accounts, though down from the €6,695 spike in August.
