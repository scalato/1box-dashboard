Occupancy by square meter declined 1.9 percentage points from August to October, falling from 70.7% to 68.8%, while unit-level occupancy remained flat at 64.1% in both September and October despite modest net rental activity of 1–3 units per month. Revenue contracted 1.0% over the period (from €32,180 to €31,849), driven by a 2.0% drop in average rate per square meter from €18.10 to €17.74. Arrears deteriorated meaningfully, climbing from €6,695 across 19 accounts in August to €6,656 across 37 accounts by October, signaling a potential collection challenge despite a slight reduction in total outstanding amount.
