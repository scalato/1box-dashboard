Occupancy at 1BOX Sittard has declined steadily over the three-month period, dropping from 68.1% unit occupancy in May to 62.2% in July—a loss of 18 rented units and 5.9 percentage points. Rental velocity has turned negative, with net losses of 8 units in June and 10 units in July, driven by elevated vacate rates (17–19 per month) against modest move-ins (7–9 per month). Revenue has remained relatively flat despite declining occupancy, ranging from €31,019 to €32,598 monthly, while per-sqm rates held steady around €16.85–€16.96. Arrears spiked to €5,765 across 33 accounts in June before falling to €4,257 across 25 accounts in July, suggesting some collection activity but continued cash-flow pressure.
