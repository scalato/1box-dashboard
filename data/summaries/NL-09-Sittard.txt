Occupancy declined from June through July, falling 3.3 percentage points by unit count before stabilizing and recovering slightly in August to 63.2%, still 2.3 points below June. The site reversed course on rental velocity in August with a net gain of 3 units after two consecutive months of losses (net -8 in June, net -10 in July), driven by a sharp drop in vacates from 19 to 7. Revenue per square meter improved notably to 18.10 in August from 16.85 in July, though overall monthly revenue remained flat around 31,000–33,000. Arrears count improved steadily from 33 accounts to 19, but the outstanding balance spiked to 6,695 in August despite fewer delinquent accounts, signaling some high-value arrears cases.
