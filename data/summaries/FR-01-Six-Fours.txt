Occupancy by unit count declined from 84.2% in June to 81.3% in August, a loss of nearly 3 percentage points, driven by consistent negative rental velocity with net losses of 2–5 units each month. Despite falling occupancy, revenue grew 3.8% over the period from $51,551 to $53,507, supported by rate increases that pushed average rent per sqm from $22.28 to $23.12. Arrears spiked sharply in August to $66,409 across 44 accounts, more than double the July level, signaling a deterioration in collection quality that warrants immediate attention.
