Occupancy has improved steadily over the three-month period, rising from 46.1% to 48.7% by unit count and from 64.3% to 66.5% by square meter, driven by positive net rentals in September (net +3) and October (net +8) after a weak August (net −6). Revenue has declined despite occupancy gains, falling from €24,272 in August to €22,946 in October, as the average rate per square meter dropped 7.6% from €12.81 to €11.83. Arrears remain elevated at €2,172 across 19 accounts as of October, though both the outstanding balance and account count have trended down since September's peak of €2,400 across 39 accounts.
