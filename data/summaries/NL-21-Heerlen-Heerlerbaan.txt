Occupancy has declined sharply since May, with unit occupancy falling from 49.7% to 47.6% and square-meter occupancy dropping 5.3 percentage points to 65.6% in July. Rental velocity deteriorated significantly in July, posting net negative 11 units after modest positive momentum in May and June, driven by a spike in vacates to 17 units against only 6 move-ins. Despite occupancy pressure, revenue per square meter climbed steadily from 11.13 to 11.96, offsetting some volume loss, though absolute revenue growth has stalled between June and July. Arrears count rose to 27 accounts in July, though outstanding arrears amount decreased slightly, warranting closer monitoring of collection performance.
