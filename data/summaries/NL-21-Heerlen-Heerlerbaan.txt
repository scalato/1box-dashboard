Occupancy at 1BOX Heerlen-Heerlerbaan stabilized in September after declining through July and August, with unit occupancy recovering from 46.1% in August to 46.8% in September following a net gain of three rentals. Rental velocity improved notably in September with 18 move-ins (versus 6–8 in prior months), reversing the site's previous net rental losses of 11 and 6 units respectively. Revenue remained relatively flat across the three months at approximately €23,000–24,000 monthly despite rate-per-sqm fluctuations, though arrears count increased to 39 accounts in September. The improving move-in activity and stabilized occupancy suggest a potential turnaround, though the rising arrears count warrants monitoring.
