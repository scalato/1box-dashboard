Occupancy at 1BOX Heerlen-Heerlerbaan declined from June through August, falling from 50.5% to 46.1% by unit and 70.2% to 64.3% by square metre, driven by net negative rentals of 11 units in July and 6 units in August. Rental velocity remains weak with move-ins consistently low (6–12 per month) while vacates have stayed elevated at 9–17 per month. Revenue held relatively flat across the period despite occupancy pressure, rising slightly from €23,136 to €24,272, supported by rate increases from €11.62 to €12.81 per square metre. Arrears count rose from 16 to 29 instances, though outstanding arrears balance improved significantly from €3,549 to €1,996 in August, suggesting collection efforts or partial resolution of earlier balances.
