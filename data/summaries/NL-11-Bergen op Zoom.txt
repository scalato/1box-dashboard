Occupancy at 1BOX Bergen op Zoom has declined modestly over the three-month period, with square-meter occupancy falling from 72.2% in August to 69.1% in October, though unit occupancy has stabilized at 62.9% since September. Rental velocity has improved materially, moving from negative 7 net units in August to breakeven in October, indicating the site has arrested its unit loss. Revenue has declined slightly from €23,893 to €23,165 despite a modest increase in average rate per square meter (from €17.47 to €17.78), reflecting the occupancy headwind. Arrears have improved meaningfully, dropping from €5,416 across 41 accounts in August to €5,101 across 19 accounts in October, signaling better payment discipline.
