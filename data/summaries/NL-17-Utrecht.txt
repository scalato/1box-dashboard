Occupancy at 1BOX Utrecht remained essentially flat across the three-month window, fluctuating between 65.8% and 68.0% on a unit basis, with sqm occupancy peaking in September at 70.6% before declining slightly to 69.5% in October. Rental velocity weakened notably: after a strong net gain of 12 units in September, the site added only 1 net unit in October, with move-ins dropping from 24 to 19. Revenue declined 1% from September to October despite a modest improvement in rent-per-sqm (from €14.31 to €14.65), and arrears have grown consistently across the period, rising from €6,213 to €7,705 with delinquent account counts increasing from 53 to 69.
