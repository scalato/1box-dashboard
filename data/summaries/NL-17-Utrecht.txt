1BOX Utrecht has experienced steady occupancy decline over the past three months, with unit occupancy falling from 68.9% in June to 65.8% in August and square-meter occupancy dropping from 73.4% to 69.0% across the same period. Net rentals have remained negative each month (−9, −11, and −5 units respectively), though the rate of loss slowed in August as vacates declined. Revenue held relatively flat at approximately 41,000 despite occupancy pressure, supported by a 5.6% increase in average rate per square meter from 13.36 to 14.11. Arrears outstanding spiked in August to 6,213 across 53 accounts, up sharply from the 4,352 low recorded in July, signaling a potential collection risk that warrants monitoring.
