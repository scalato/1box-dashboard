1BOX Utrecht experienced a steady decline in occupancy over the three-month window, with unit occupancy falling from 71.0% in May to 66.7% in July, driven by negative net rentals of -9 and -11 units in June and July respectively. Revenue remained relatively flat at approximately €41,000 monthly despite the occupancy loss, as average rent per square meter increased from €12.96 to €13.57, suggesting rate optimization is offsetting volume headwinds. Arrears improved in July to €4,352 across 44 accounts, down from a peak of €5,431 in June, indicating some collection success though still elevated from May levels. The combination of declining unit count and persistent negative rental velocity warrants attention to leasing velocity and competitive positioning.
