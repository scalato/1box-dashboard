1BOX Utrecht reversed its occupancy decline in September, recovering to 67.9% unit occupancy (up 2.1 points from the July low of 66.7%), driven by net rentals turning positive with a gain of 12 units after two months of consecutive losses. Revenue per square meter improved steadily across the period from 13.57 to 14.31, though absolute net revenue remained essentially flat around 42k monthly. Arrears are a growing concern, rising from 4.4k outstanding across 44 accounts in July to 6.6k across 62 accounts by September, suggesting collection friction despite the occupancy recovery.
