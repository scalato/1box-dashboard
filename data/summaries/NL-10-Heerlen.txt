Occupancy by unit count rose steadily from 81.4% in July to 84.2% in September, with net additions of 5 units in August and 2 units in September, though September saw elevated vacates (9 units) that partially offset move-in activity. Revenue grew 4.8% over the period from €19,250 to €20,168, driven by modest rate increases from €15.04 to €15.22 per sqm. Arrears have risen notably from €748 to €1,160 outstanding, a concerning 55% increase that warrants attention despite relatively stable arrears account counts.
