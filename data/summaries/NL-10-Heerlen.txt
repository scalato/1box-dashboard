Occupancy by unit count held steady at 84.2% from September through October, while square-meter occupancy fluctuated between 85.0% and 86.5%, ending slightly higher in October. Rental velocity has slowed considerably, declining from net +5 units in August to just +1 unit in October, indicating weaker demand momentum. Revenue remained relatively flat across the three months (€19,382–€20,168), with rates per square meter holding around €15.19–€15.22. Arrears improved sharply in October, dropping from €1,160.04 outstanding across 17 accounts to €288.83 across 8 accounts, a significant positive shift in collections.
