Occupancy at 1BOX Heerlen declined 2.5 points by unit percentage from June to July before recovering to 83.6% in August, just 0.5 points below the starting level. Net rentals turned negative in July with a loss of 3 units, then rebounded strongly in August with a net gain of 5 units as move-ins accelerated to 9 against 5 vacates. Revenue grew modestly from €18,846 in June to €19,382 in August while rate per sqm improved from €14.56 to €15.05 across the period. Arrears outstanding has fluctuated, declining to €748 in July before rising back to €950 in August, though arrears count remained stable at 16 in both June and August.
