1BOX Heerlen shows mixed momentum across the three-month window. Unit occupancy peaked in June at 83.1% before slipping back to 81.4% in July, while square-meter occupancy declined more sharply from 86.5% to 82.5%, suggesting larger units are vacant. Rental velocity turned negative in July with net minus 3 units after a modest plus 3 in June, driven by a spike in vacates to 6 units against only 3 move-ins. Revenue per square meter improved consistently from €14.24 to €15.04, but this gain masks underlying occupancy weakness; arrears also merit attention, rising to €747.97 outstanding across 18 accounts as of July, up from 10 accounts in May.
