1BOX Groningen showed mixed momentum over the three-month period. Unit occupancy peaked at 82.5% in August before declining 2.9 percentage points to 79.6% by September, driven by a net loss of 10 units that month after a strong net gain of 10 in August. Revenue continued climbing despite occupancy weakness, rising from 30,555 euros in July to 33,159 euros in September, supported by rate increases from 20.9 to 21.87 euros per sqm. Arrears worsened notably, with outstanding balances jumping from 2,464 euros across 22 accounts in July to 3,941 euros in September, signaling potential collection pressure ahead.
