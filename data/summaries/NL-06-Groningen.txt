Occupancy by square meters declined from 80.2% in May to 76.8% in July, a 3.4-point drop over the three-month span, while unit-based occupancy held relatively steady at 78.6% to 80.0%. Net rental activity was minimal, with only 2 net units added across the full period despite move-ins accelerating from 18 to 24 per month; July saw a net loss of 1 unit as vacates exceeded move-ins. Revenue declined 2.6% from May to July to €30,555, and average rent per square meter fell from €21.25 to €20.90, though arrears improved meaningfully with outstanding balances dropping 52% and account count declining from 34 to 22.
