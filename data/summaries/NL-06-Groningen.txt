Occupancy at 1BOX Groningen has deteriorated significantly over the three-month period, with unit occupancy falling from 82.5% in August to 75.5% in October, a decline of 7 percentage points driven by consistent negative rental velocity of net -10 units in September and net -17 units in October. Move-ins have slowed sharply from 26 in August to just 10 in October, while vacates have remained elevated at 27-29 per month. Despite the occupancy erosion, revenue per square meter has climbed from 21.51 to 22.88, offsetting some of the volume loss through rate increases, though total revenue has only grown marginally to 33,486. Arrears increased notably from 2,865 across 16 accounts in August to a peak of 3,941 across 22 accounts in September, though the count has moderated slightly to 19 accounts by October.
