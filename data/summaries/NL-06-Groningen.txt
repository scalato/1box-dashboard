Occupancy at 1BOX Groningen declined in July to 76.8% square-meter occupancy before recovering strongly in August to 81.3%, a net gain of 2.5 percentage points across the three-month window. Rental velocity turned positive in August with net +10 units after a soft July (net -1), driven by 26 move-ins against only 17 vacates. Revenue improved to €32,657 in August, the highest of the period, with rent-per-sqm rising to €21.51, while arrears declined to €2,865 outstanding across 16 accounts, the lowest count in the span.
