Occupancy at 1BOX Barendrecht declined from June through July (unit occupancy fell from 77.2% to 75.9%), then stabilized in August at 75.9%, while square-meter occupancy recovered slightly to 80.3%. Net rentals were negative in both June and July (net -5 and -7 units respectively) before reaching equilibrium in August with 18 move-ins matching 18 vacates. Revenue grew steadily across the period, rising from €63,040 in June to €65,684 in August, driven by rate increases from €18.65 to €19.32 per sqm. Arrears outstanding spiked to €7,579 in August after declining in July, with the count stabilizing around 34–43 accounts in arrears.
