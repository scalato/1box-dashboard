Occupancy by square meter has remained stable at around 80% across the three-month window, though unit-level occupancy has drifted slightly downward from 75.9% in July to 75.5% in September. Rental velocity has been weak, with net rentals of -7 units in July, flat in August, and -3 units in September, resulting in a net loss of three rented units overall. Revenue has been essentially flat between July and September (ranging from 64,206 to 65,684), while rent-per-sqm has softened slightly from 19.26 to 18.88. Arrears have risen notably from 6,618 to 8,861 outstanding, though the number of accounts in arrears has decreased from 43 to 33, suggesting concentration among fewer tenants.
