1BOX Barendrecht showed recovery in October after a soft September. Unit occupancy declined slightly from 75.9% to 75.5% in September (net -3 rentals), then rebounded to 76.7% in October with net +8 rentals driven by a spike in move-ins to 31 units. Revenue improved from €65,442 in September to €67,441 in October, with rent-per-sqm rising to €19.54, though occupancy-by-sqm gained only 1.7 percentage points across the full three-month span. Arrears outstanding increased to €9,251, up 22% since August despite a slight decline in account count, suggesting higher past-due balances per delinquent customer.
