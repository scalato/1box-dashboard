Occupancy at Amsterdam-Schepenbergweg has improved steadily across the three-month window, rising from 43.9% to 45.1% by unit count and 38.8% to 40.8% by square meter, driven by net positive rental activity (net +7 units in August, +3 in September). Revenue has remained relatively flat month-over-month despite occupancy gains, with average rent per square meter declining from 15.87 in August to 14.95 in September, suggesting a shift toward smaller or lower-priced units. Arrears outstanding fell significantly from 9,118 in August to 5,617 in September, a favorable shift that partially offsets the modest revenue pressure from rate compression.
