Occupancy showed steady improvement over the quarter, with unit occupancy rising from 44.7% in August to 47.4% in October, while square-meter occupancy climbed from 39.6% to 42.6%. October marked a significant acceleration in net rentals with 16 units added—more than double September's gain of 3 units—driven by strong move-ins (29) and notably low vacates (15). Revenue recovered to 42,498 in October after dipping slightly in September, though the average rate per square meter declined from 15.87 in August to 15.73 in October. Arrears decreased substantially from 9,117 in August to 7,340 in October despite the count remaining flat at 39 accounts.
