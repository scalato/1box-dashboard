The site showed stagnation through July before gaining momentum in August, with occupancy edging up from 43.9% to 44.7% by unit count and net rentals turning positive at 7 units in August after two flat months. Revenue grew steadily across the period, rising from €39,749 in June to €41,491 in August, while rent-per-sqm improved from €15.16 to €15.87. Arrears remain a concern, hovering around €9,100 across the last two months with 35–36 accounts in default, though the account count declined slightly from 46 in June.
