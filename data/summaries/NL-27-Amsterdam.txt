Occupancy by square metre has declined 1.6 percentage points from May to July, dropping from 40.4% to 38.8%, while unit occupancy has held flat at 43.9% since June despite zero net rentals in both June and July. Revenue has increased modestly from €37,814 in May to €40,141 in July, driven by a rising average rate per square metre that climbed from €14.56 to €15.23. Arrears have improved overall, falling from €9,922 across 53 accounts in May to €9,111 across 35 accounts in July, indicating better collection despite ongoing payment issues.
