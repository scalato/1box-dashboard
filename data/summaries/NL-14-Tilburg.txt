1BOX Tilburg has remained relatively flat on occupancy over the three-month period, holding steady around 75-76% despite mixed rental activity: June saw net losses of 11 units, July recovered with net gains of 5 units, and August declined again by 3 units, leaving the site at 506 rented units as of month-end. Revenue has grown from €69,001 in June to €74,010 in August, driven primarily by rate increases (€14.79 to €16.03 per sqm) rather than volume gains. Arrears are a concern, nearly doubling from 40 accounts in June to 85 accounts in August, though the total outstanding amount dipped to €15,100 in August after peaking at €22,698 in July, suggesting inconsistent payment behavior.
