Occupancy at 1BOX Tilburg improved notably in October, rising to 77.3% unit occupancy from 75.1% in September, driven by a strong shift in rental dynamics with net +16 units added versus net losses in the prior two months. Move-ins accelerated to 40 units in October while vacates dropped to 27, reversing the negative momentum seen through August and September. Revenue remained relatively flat across the three months despite occupancy gains, with the average rate per square meter declining slightly from 16.03 in August to 15.52 in October. Arrears improved significantly over the period, declining from 15,099.93 to 10,439.82 outstanding and from 85 to 68 accounts in arrears.
