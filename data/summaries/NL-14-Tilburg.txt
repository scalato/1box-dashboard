1BOX Tilburg has experienced softening momentum over the three-month period from July to September. Unit occupancy declined from 76.1% to 75.1%, with negative net rentals in both August (–3 units) and September (–2 units), though square-meter occupancy stabilized around 74–75% by month-end. Revenue fell 3.4% from July to September (€71,850 to €69,404), and average rate per square meter slipped from €15.85 to €15.76. Arrears have improved significantly, dropping from €22,698 (53 accounts) in July to €5,855 (80 accounts) in September, suggesting improved collections despite softer rental activity.
