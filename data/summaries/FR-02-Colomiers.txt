Occupancy showed steady improvement over the quarter, rising from 67.8% unit occupancy in August to 71.0% in October, with sqm occupancy climbing 2.5 points from 71.9% to 74.4%. Net rentals rebounded sharply to +20 units in October after stalling in September, driven by 35 move-ins against only 16 vacates. Revenue grew modestly from 38,190 to 39,993, while rate-per-sqm stabilized at 12.82 in both September and October. Arrears showed a positive trend, declining from 48,253 outstanding across 66 accounts in August to 46,418 across just 26 accounts by October, indicating improved collections and reduced delinquency.
