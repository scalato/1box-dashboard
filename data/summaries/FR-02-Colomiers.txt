1BOX Colomiers has shown steady occupancy growth over the three-month period, rising from 61.5% unit occupancy in May to 65.1% in July, with particularly strong rental momentum in July when the site added 13 net units. Revenue increased 4.0% from May to July to €35,721, supported by a modest uptick in rate-per-sqm from €12.29 to €12.44, though June saw a slight revenue dip despite occupancy gains. Arrears have ticked up in July to €47,179 across 85 accounts after improving in June, warranting monitoring alongside the positive occupancy trajectory.
