Occupancy held relatively steady across the three-month window, rising from 65.1% to 67.9% by unit count through August before plateauing in September, though square-meter occupancy dipped slightly in the final month to 70.4%. Rental velocity slowed considerably in September with zero net rentals (29 move-ins versus 28 vacates) compared to gains of 13 and 16 units in July and August respectively. Revenue remained stable between €35.7k and €38.4k monthly, with the rate per square meter climbing to €12.82 in September from €12.41–€12.44 in prior months. Arrears showed meaningful improvement, dropping from 85 accounts outstanding in July to just 33 in September, with total arrears amount remaining stable around €47–€48k.
