1BOX Eindhoven Best stabilized in August after two months of occupancy decline. Unit occupancy bottomed at 76.5% in July, then recovered to 78.1% in August, driven by a reversal to positive net rentals of 5 units (versus net losses of 14 and 11 in June and July). Revenue remained flat around €30,200–31,150 across the three months despite occupancy swings, as the average rate per sqm held steady near €22.2. Arrears improved materially, dropping from €1,387 in June to €389 in July before rising modestly to €714 in August, though the count remained low at 9 accounts.
