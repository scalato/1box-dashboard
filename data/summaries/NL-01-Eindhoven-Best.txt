Occupancy at 1BOX Eindhoven Best has strengthened consistently over the three-month period, rising from 76.5% unit occupancy in July to 81.8% in September, driven by improving rental velocity—the site swung from net -11 units in July to net +11 units in September as move-ins accelerated and vacates declined. Revenue has remained stable at approximately €30,600 monthly despite a 6% decline in average rate per sqm from €22.39 to €21.09, suggesting volume gains offset pricing pressure. Arrears outstanding have grown notably from €390 to €1,268, though the count of accounts in arrears actually decreased from 12 to 8, indicating concentration among fewer tenants.
