Occupancy has climbed steadily over the three-month period, with unit occupancy rising from 78.1% in August to 84.4% in October, driven by consistent net rental gains of 5 to 11 units per month and vacate rates that have remained modest. Revenue grew 3.3% from August to October (€30,595 to €31,599), though average rate per square meter declined slightly from €22.05 to €20.98, indicating occupancy gains came at slightly lower pricing. Arrears outstanding increased to €1,131.36 by October, up from €714.25 in August, though the count of accounts in arrears held steady at 8 in both September and October.
