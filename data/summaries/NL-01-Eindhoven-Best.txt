Occupancy at 1BOX Eindhoven Best has declined steadily over the three-month period, falling from 85.0% unit occupancy in May to 76.5% in July, a loss of 8.5 percentage points. Rental velocity has been negative, with the site losing 14 net units in June and 11 more in July, driven by elevated vacate activity (22 and 19 respectively) that outpaced move-ins. Revenue has tracked the occupancy decline, dropping from €32,113 in May to €30,209 in July, while rent-per-sqm has remained relatively stable around €22.30–€22.43. Arrears have improved significantly, declining from €3,048 across 29 accounts in May to €390 across 12 accounts in July, suggesting better collection rather than operational recovery.
