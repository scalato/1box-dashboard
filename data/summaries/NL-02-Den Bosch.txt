Occupancy recovered in October after two months of decline, with unit occupancy rising from 73.2% in September to 75.1%, and square-meter occupancy climbing to 78.6%. Rental velocity turned positive in October with net +9 units rented, a reversal from the negative trends in August and September. Revenue remained relatively flat across the three months (ranging from €52,127 to €53,914 monthly), while average rate per square meter edged down slightly from €22.17 in August to €21.82 in October. Arrears outstanding declined significantly from €5,954 in August to €3,438 in October, though arrears count surged to 197 accounts in September before moderating to 98 in October, suggesting improved collections and customer payment behavior.
