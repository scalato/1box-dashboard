Occupancy declined across the three-month window, with unit occupancy falling from 76.2% in May to 74.5% in July, driven by net negative rentals of -3 units in May and -8 units in July despite moderate move-in activity. Revenue per square meter improved notably, rising from 20.06 in May to 22.32 in July, though overall net revenue growth was modest at approximately 7.8% across the span. Arrears fluctuated, peaking at 5,521 outstanding in June before declining to 3,607 by July, with arrear account counts trending down from 18 to 13 accounts. The site is experiencing occupancy headwinds with vacates outpacing move-ins in both May and July, though rate increases and lower arrear balances suggest some operational stabilization in July.
