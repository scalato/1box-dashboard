Occupancy declined modestly across the three-month span, dropping from 74.5% to 73.2% by unit count and 78.2% to 77.6% by square meter, driven by consistent net negative rentals each month (minus 8, minus 2, and minus 3 units respectively). Revenue remained relatively flat at approximately 52,400–53,900 per month, though average rate per square meter fell from 22.32 to 21.76. Arrears activity is volatile and warrants attention: the count surged dramatically to 197 accounts in September while outstanding arrears balance improved to 2,924, suggesting a large number of smaller delinquencies rather than concentrated high-value defaults.
