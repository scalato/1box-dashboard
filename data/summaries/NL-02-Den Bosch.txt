Occupancy has declined steadily over the three-month period, dropping from 76.2% to 74.0% in unit terms and from 78.8% to 77.5% by square meter, with net negative rentals of 8 units in July followed by 2 units in August offsetting modest move-in activity. Revenue has remained relatively flat at approximately €52,400 in July and August despite the occupancy headwind, supported by a rate increase from €20.52 to €22.17 per square meter. Arrears are a growing concern, rising from €3,607 with 13 accounts in arrears during July to €5,954 across 23 accounts in August, suggesting collection pressure is mounting.
