1BOX Alkmaar recovered from a weak July, with occupancy rising 1.9 percentage points by unit through August before stabilizing at 80.3% in September. Net rentals turned positive in August with +6 units and remained modest in September with +1 unit, indicating steady but slow tenant acquisition offsetting natural attrition. Revenue grew incrementally from €50.5k to €51.1k across the three months while per-sqm rates held relatively flat around €20, suggesting growth is driven by volume rather than pricing power. Arrears spiked sharply in August to €17.7k before settling to €12.4k in September, warranting continued monitoring.
