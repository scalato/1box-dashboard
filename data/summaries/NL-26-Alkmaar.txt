Occupancy has trended upward over the three-month span, rising from 80.0% to 81.1% by unit and from 84.2% to 85.3% by square meter, with the strongest gain occurring in October. Rental velocity remains modest, with net additions of 6, 1, and 3 units across August, September, and October respectively, though October showed improved move-in activity at 17 units. Revenue grew steadily from €50,681 to €51,655, while rate-per-sqm increased from €19.96 to €20.27. Arrears have improved significantly, declining from €17,666 to €6,186 despite a slight uptick in arrears account count, indicating stronger collections.
