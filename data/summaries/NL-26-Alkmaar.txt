1BOX Alkmaar's occupancy declined steadily over the three-month period, dropping from 81.6% unit occupancy in May to 78.4% in July, driven by consistent negative net rentals of 2 to 7 units per month. Revenue remained relatively flat despite occupancy pressure, holding between €49,893 and €50,525, supported by a 3.7% increase in average rate per sqm from €19.52 to €20.24. Arrears showed marked improvement in July, plummeting to €6,291 from around €24,750 in prior months, with delinquent account count falling from 32 to 11. The site is shedding occupancy while maintaining pricing power, though the ongoing negative rental velocity suggests leasing challenges that require attention.
