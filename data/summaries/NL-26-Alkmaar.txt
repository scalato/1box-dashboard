Occupancy at 1BOX Alkmaar declined from 80.2% to 78.4% between June and July before recovering to 80.0% in August, with unit occupancy essentially flat across the three-month span. Rental velocity turned positive in August with net +6 units after two consecutive months of net losses, driven by a reduction in vacates from 14 to 9 units. Revenue remained stable around €50,500 monthly, though the average rate per square meter dipped slightly from €20.24 in July to €19.96 in August. Arrears showed substantial improvement, dropping from €24,751 in June to €6,291 in July, though rising again to €17,666 in August with 10 accounts delinquent.
