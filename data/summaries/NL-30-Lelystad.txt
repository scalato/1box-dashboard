Occupancy at 1BOX Lelystad declined slightly from August to September (76.7% to 75.7% by unit count) before recovering modestly in October to 76.4%, driven by improved rental velocity with net +4 units in October versus net -6 units in September. Revenue grew steadily across the three months, rising from €55,004 in August to €57,165 in October, while average rent per square meter increased from €17.26 to €18.81. Arrears improved consistently, falling from €9,712 across 70 accounts in August to €7,987 across 61 accounts in October, indicating better collection and a shrinking problem portfolio.
