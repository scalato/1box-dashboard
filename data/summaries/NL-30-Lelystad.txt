Occupancy at 1BOX Lelystad has declined steadily over the three-month period, dropping from 80.8% unit occupancy in May to 76.9% in July, a loss of 22 rented units driven by high vacate activity outpacing move-ins (net -7 units in June, net -15 units in July). Revenue per square meter has improved from €16.28 to €17.35, partially offsetting the occupancy pressure, yet total net revenue growth has been modest. Arrears have risen from €5,889 to €7,664 across 41–53 accounts, indicating a potential collection risk alongside the occupancy headwind.
