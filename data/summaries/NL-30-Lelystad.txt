Occupancy at 1BOX Lelystad has declined steadily over the three-month period, dropping from 79.6% unit occupancy in June to 76.7% in August, representing a 2.9 percentage point loss. The site has experienced consistent negative net rentals, losing 23 units across the span (net -7 in June, -15 in July, -1 in August), though the bleeding slowed significantly in August with move-ins reaching 18 units. Revenue has remained relatively flat at approximately €55,000 monthly despite the occupancy decline, supported by a modest rate increase from €16.38 to €17.26 per sqm. Arrears have deteriorated noticeably, climbing from €7,045 outstanding across 45 accounts in June to €9,712 across 70 accounts in August, signaling both rising delinquency count and dollar exposure.
