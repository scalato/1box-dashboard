Occupancy at 1BOX Lelystad has declined steadily over the three-month period, dropping from 76.9% unit occupancy in July to 75.7% in September, driven by consistent net negative rentals each month (−15, −1, and −6 units respectively). Revenue has remained relatively flat at approximately €55,000 monthly despite the occupancy slide, though the rate per sqm improved to €18.20 in September from €17.35 in July. Arrears have become a concern, rising to €9,711 outstanding across 70 accounts in August before moderating slightly to €9,036 across 65 accounts in September, indicating emerging collection pressure that warrants monitoring.
