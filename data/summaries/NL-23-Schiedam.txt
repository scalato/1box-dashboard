Occupancy stabilized in August after declining through July, with unit occupancy holding at 79.5% (up 0.4 points from July's 79.1%), though still down 1.3 points from June's 80.8%. Rental velocity improved markedly, shifting from a loss of 8 units in July to a net gain of 2 units in August as move-ins accelerated to 19 against 17 vacates. Revenue dipped slightly in August to €80,266 from July's €82,089, while per-square-meter rates remained stable around €26.1–26.4; however, arrears increased to €16,012 across 28 accounts, up from €13,374 in July, warranting attention to collection efforts.
