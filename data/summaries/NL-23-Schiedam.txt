Occupancy declined slightly from August to September, falling 1.3 percentage points on a unit basis, before recovering partially in October to 78.9%, though still below August's 79.5%. Rental velocity turned negative in September with net minus 7 units, but returned to positive in October with net plus 4 units, indicating stabilization. Revenue remained relatively flat across the period at approximately 80,300 euros monthly, while per-square-meter rates climbed steadily from 26.13 to 26.79. Arrears outstanding rose noticeably from 16,012 euros in August to 20,651 euros in October, with account counts fluctuating between 28 and 42 delinquent customers, flagging a growing collection challenge.
