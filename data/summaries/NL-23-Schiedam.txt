Occupancy at 1BOX Schiedam declined over the three-month period, with square-meter occupancy falling from 85.9% in July to 84.1% in September, and unit occupancy dropping from 79.1% to 78.2%. Rental velocity turned negative, with net losses of 8 units in July and 7 units in September, offset only by a modest gain of 2 units in August. Revenue remained relatively flat around €80,000–€82,000 monthly despite the occupancy slide, as rate-per-sqm held steady near €26. Arrears are a growing concern, with outstanding balances rising from €13,374 in July to €16,620 in September and delinquent account count nearly doubling from 19 to 42.
