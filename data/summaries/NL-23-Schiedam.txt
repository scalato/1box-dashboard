1BOX Schiedam experienced a sharp occupancy decline across the three-month window, dropping from 83.9% unit occupancy in May to 79.1% in July, driven by net negative rentals of 18 units in June and 8 units in July. Revenue per square meter recovered strongly in July to €26.40 from €24.02 in June, offsetting some occupancy loss and lifting total net revenue to €82,089. Arrears have improved meaningfully, falling from €17,884 across 60 accounts in May to €13,374 across 19 accounts in July, indicating better collections despite the occupancy headwind.
