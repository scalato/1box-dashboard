Occupancy by unit count declined from 84.0% in May to 83.3% in June before recovering to 84.1% in July, while square-meter occupancy remained relatively flat around 86–87%. The site shifted from negative net rentals in May and June (minus 5 and minus 4 units respectively) to positive momentum in July with net plus 6 units, driven by 38 move-ins against 32 vacates. Revenue remained stable in the €76,800–77,100 range across the three months, with average rent per square meter rising modestly from €23.25 to €23.76. Arrears outstanding improved notably from €20,567 in June to €12,982 in July, though the count of accounts in arrears stayed between 37–66.
