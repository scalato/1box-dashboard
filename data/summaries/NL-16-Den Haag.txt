1BOX Den Haag has shown steady occupancy growth over the three-month span, with unit occupancy rising from 84.1% in July to 86.0% in September, while square-meter occupancy climbed 3.0 percentage points to 89.1%. Net rentals have remained consistent at 6-7 units per month, with 13 net units added across the period and rental rates increasing modestly from €23.76 to €24.29 per square meter, driving revenue up 6.9% to €82,133 in September. Arrears have escalated noticeably, however—outstanding amount nearly jumped 51% from €12,982 in July to €19,530 in September, and arrears accounts tripled from 38 to 115 over the same window, signaling a meaningful deterioration in payment behavior despite operational gains.
