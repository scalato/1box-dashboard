Occupancy has steadily improved over the three-month period, rising from 83.3% to 84.9% by unit count and 86.9% to 87.0% by square meter, driven by consistent positive net rentals of 6 units in both July and August following a net loss of 4 units in June. Revenue and rent-per-sqm have both trended upward, with August net revenue reaching 79,843 euros (up 3.9% from June) and average rate per sqm climbing to 24.12 from 23.61. Arrears outstanding fell sharply from 20,567 euros in June to roughly 13,000 euros in July and August, though arrears count increased significantly in August to 70 accounts, warranting monitoring.
