Occupancy peaked in September at 86.0% unit occupancy before declining to 83.9% in October, a drop of 2.1 percentage points driven by net negative rentals of 15 units in October after two consecutive months of modest gains. Rental velocity has slowed sharply, with move-ins falling from 45 in August to just 26 in October while vacates remained elevated at 42 in October. Despite occupancy headwinds, revenue has edged upward from €79,843 to €83,072 across the three months, supported by rate-per-sqm growth from €24.12 to €24.86. Arrears spiked significantly in September to €19,530 across 115 accounts but have since moderated to €15,502, though the arrears count remains elevated at 89 accounts compared to 70 in August.
