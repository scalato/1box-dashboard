Occupancy has edged upward across the three-month span, with unit occupancy rising from 50.5% in June to 51.6% in August, while square-meter occupancy increased 1.5 percentage points to 55.5%. Rental velocity has been modest, with net additions slowing to just 1 unit in July before recovering slightly to 5 units in August, indicating a generally flat absorption pace. Revenue jumped 38% in August to €52,164, driven primarily by a significant spike in net revenue rather than rate expansion, which remained stable at €12.38 per sqm. Arrears declined notably from €43,597 in June to €43,161 in August despite a slight uptick from July, with the arrears count dropping sharply to 10 accounts in August, suggesting improved collections.
