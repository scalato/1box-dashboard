1BOX Mérignac has shown steady occupancy growth over the three-month period, rising from 50.8% to 53.9% on a unit basis and 54.5% to 58.7% by square meter, driven by accelerating net rentals that peaked at 16 units in September. Rental velocity improved notably, with move-ins climbing to 32 in September while vacates dropped to 13, compared to near-parity between inflows and outflows in July. Revenue per square meter edged up from 12.34 to 12.56, though total net revenue dipped in September despite higher occupancy, and arrears outstanding increased to 50,388 across 17 accounts, suggesting collection challenges despite better unit-level performance.
