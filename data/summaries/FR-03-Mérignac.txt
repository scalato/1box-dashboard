Occupancy at 1BOX Mérignac has risen modestly from 47.7% to 50.8% by unit count over the three-month span, driven by strong net rentals of 19 units in June but stalling at just 1 net unit in July as move-ins slowed and vacates increased. Revenue has remained relatively flat at approximately €37,500 monthly despite occupancy gains, though per-square-meter rates improved from €11.86 to €12.34, suggesting modest pricing power. Arrears spiked in June to €43,597 across 23 accounts but have since receded to €39,533, indicating improved collections in July despite the slowdown in leasing activity.
