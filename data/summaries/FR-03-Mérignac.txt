Occupancy at 1BOX Mérignac has climbed steadily over the three-month period, rising from 51.6% to 56.5% by unit count and 55.5% to 60.3% by square meter, driven by consistent net rental gains of +5, +16, and +18 units respectively across August, September, and October. Rental velocity has accelerated, with move-ins increasing from 27 to 41 per month while maintaining controlled vacates, indicating strong leasing momentum. Revenue per square meter improved modestly from 12.38 to 12.77, though monthly net revenue has remained relatively flat around 42–52k due to the ongoing occupancy ramp-up phase. A notable flag: arrears count jumped from 10 to 23 accounts despite outstanding balance dropping sharply to 2,950.97 in October, suggesting recent collections activity or account restructuring rather than fundamental credit quality improvement.
