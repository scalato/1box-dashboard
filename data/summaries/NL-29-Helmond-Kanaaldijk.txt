The site added 9 net units across May and June before accelerating to 7 net rentals in July, with unit occupancy climbing from 37.2% to 38.7% despite a dip in move-ins last month. Square-meter occupancy peaked in June at 56.0% then softened slightly to 55.2% in July, while revenue remained flat around €35k monthly despite rate-per-sqm declining from €11.54 to €11.34. Arrears spiked to nearly €8k in June before improving back to €6.8k in July, with the delinquent account count dropping from 59 to 44 over the same period, suggesting better collections discipline.
