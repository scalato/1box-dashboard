Occupancy showed mixed performance over the three-month span, rising from 38.7% to 41.4% in August before declining slightly to 41.1% in September; square-meter occupancy peaked at 59.1% in August but fell to 56.8% by September. Rental velocity slowed considerably, with net additions of 7 units in July and 16 in August giving way to a net loss of 1 unit in September as vacates (19) exceeded move-ins (18). Revenue grew from €34,794 to €38,495 across the period despite the occupancy pullback, driven by a higher average rate per square meter (€11.80 in September versus €11.34 in July). Arrears remain elevated at €6,574 across 43 accounts, though the outstanding balance and account count have improved modestly from August's peaks.
