Occupancy recovered modestly in October after a dip in September, with unit occupancy rising from 41.1% to 42.4% and sqm occupancy climbing to 58.5%, though still below August's 59.1%. Rental velocity turned positive in October with net +9 units after a flat September (-1 unit), driven by stronger move-ins (25 vs. 18 in September). Revenue remained relatively stable around 38.4k–38.5k in September and October despite occupancy fluctuations, with rates holding steady near 11.8 per sqm. Arrears have deteriorated considerably, rising from 7.1k in August to 9.6k in October across an increasing number of accounts (39 to 48), signaling a growing collection issue.
