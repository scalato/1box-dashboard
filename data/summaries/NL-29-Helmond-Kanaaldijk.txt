Occupancy improved sharply in August, rising to 41.4% on a unit basis (up 3.5 points from June) and 59.1% by square meter (up 3.1 points), driven by strong net rental activity of 16 units in August alone versus 6–7 units in prior months. The site gained 21 net units across the three-month span with move-ins consistently outpacing vacates in August (24 in, 9 out). Revenue remained stable around 35k monthly despite slight pressure on per-sqm rates, while arrears declined from 59 accounts in June to 39 in August, indicating improving collection health.
