Unit occupancy at 1BOX Rotterdam Centrum declined from 71.4% in August to 70.0% in October, with a net loss of 10 units in October alone following flat performance in September. Rental velocity weakened notably in October, recording only 16 move-ins against 27 vacates compared to a more balanced 37 move-ins and 33 vacates in August. Revenue remained relatively stable despite occupancy pressure, rising to €93,817 in October as the average rate per sqm increased to €26.50, though this may reflect seasonal rate adjustments rather than underlying demand strength. Arrears improved meaningfully across the period, declining from €19,575 across 74 accounts in August to €16,557 across 45 accounts in October, suggesting better collection performance.
