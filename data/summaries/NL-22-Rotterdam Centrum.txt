Occupancy by square meter improved from 78.9% in June to 80.3% in August, while unit-level occupancy remained relatively flat around 71%, masking underlying unit churn—the site lost 4 units net in July before recovering with a net gain of 6 units in August. Revenue grew steadily from €88,643 in June to €92,411 in August despite rate-per-sqm declining slightly, driven by the occupancy uptick. Arrears outstanding spiked sharply in August to €19,575 across 74 accounts (up from €14,667 and 59 accounts in July), signaling a potential collection or tenant quality issue worth monitoring.
