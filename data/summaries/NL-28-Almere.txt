Occupancy at 1BOX Almere has declined across the three-month span, with unit occupancy falling from 53.7% in September to 52.3% in October after peaking at that September level, while square-meter occupancy similarly dropped from 68.6% to 65.8%. Rental velocity weakened significantly in October with net negative 5 units, reversing the modest gains of August and September when the site added 12 and 9 units respectively. Revenue remained relatively stable, rising 3.1% from August to October despite occupancy pressure, supported by a rate recovery to €11.78 per sqm in October. Arrears have increased both in outstanding balance and delinquent account count over the period, growing from €4,227 across 25 accounts in August to €4,384 across 41 accounts in October, suggesting a worsening collection environment.
