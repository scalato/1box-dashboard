Occupancy at 1BOX Almere has shown steady growth over the three-month period, rising from 47.7% unit occupancy in July to 53.7% in September, with corresponding sqm occupancy climbing from 62.2% to 68.6%. Net rentals have remained positive throughout, adding 3 units in July, 12 in August, and 9 in September, though move-in activity has plateaued while vacates ticked back up in September. Revenue has grown from €17,503 to €19,964, though the rate per sqm peaked in August at €11.89 before declining slightly to €11.40 in September. Arrears are a concern, rising sharply from €3,098 across 19 accounts in July to €5,265 across 37 accounts by September, suggesting deteriorating payment collection despite occupancy gains.
