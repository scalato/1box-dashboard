Occupancy has remained relatively flat across the three-month window, holding steady at 46.9–47.7% by unit and 61.8–62.2% by square metre, though July showed modest improvement with net +3 rentals driven by stronger move-ins (12 vs 7–8 in prior months). Revenue has been stable at approximately €17,500 per month, with rate-per-sqm fluctuating between €10.87 and €11.44. Arrears have improved materially, declining from €4,114 (31 accounts) in May to €3,098 (19 accounts) in July, suggesting better payment discipline or collection efforts.
