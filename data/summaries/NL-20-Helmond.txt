1BOX Helmond showed meaningful momentum in August after a flat July, with occupancy climbing from 71.6% to 75.0% on the unit basis and 73.8% to 77.7% by square meters—a gain of 10 rented units net in August alone. Revenue remained relatively stable around €30–31k monthly despite the rate-per-sqm declining from €20.80 in July to €19.59 in August, suggesting the occupancy gains came from lower-priced unit rentals. However, arrears spiked notably in August to €7,113 across 43 accounts (up from €3,209 across 23 accounts in July), signaling a potential credit risk that warrants monitoring.
