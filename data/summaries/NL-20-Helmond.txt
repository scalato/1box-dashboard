Occupancy at 1BOX Helmond declined sharply from May to June, dropping 4.5 percentage points in unit occupancy (from 76.4% to 71.9%) driven by net negative rentals of 11 units, then stabilized in July with zero net movement. Revenue remained relatively flat across the three months despite the occupancy loss, supported by a modest increase in average rate per sqm from 19.83 to 20.80. Arrears improved significantly, declining from 4,181 outstanding across 38 accounts in May to 3,209 across 23 accounts by July, indicating better collection performance.
