Occupancy at 1BOX Helmond showed consistent upward momentum, rising from 71.6% unit occupancy in July to 78.4% by September, a gain of 6.8 percentage points driven by strong rental velocity of 10 net units in both August and September. Revenue remained stable around €31,000 monthly despite a declining average rate per square meter (from €20.80 to €19.19), indicating the occupancy gains offset pricing pressure. Arrears have escalated significantly, growing from €3,209 across 23 accounts in July to €7,734 across 53 accounts by September—a notable flag requiring collections attention.
