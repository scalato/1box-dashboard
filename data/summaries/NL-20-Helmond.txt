Occupancy at 1BOX Helmond peaked in September at 81.6% on a square-meter basis, then declined to 80.5% in October as net rentals turned negative with 7 more vacates than move-ins that month. Unit occupancy fell more sharply from 78.4% in September to 75.7% in October, erasing the prior month's gains and dropping below August's 75.0%. Revenue remained relatively stable across the period (€30,483 to €31,776), though this masked a concerning shift in October when rental velocity reversed sharply, with only 6 move-ins against 13 vacates compared to roughly balanced activity in the prior two months. Arrears improved notably in October to €5,448 across 37 accounts, down from €7,734 in September, suggesting better collection efforts despite the occupancy headwind.
