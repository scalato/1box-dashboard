Occupancy declined sharply in July, falling from 78.4% to 73.9% unit occupancy as vacates outpaced move-ins by six units, but recovered partially in August with net rentals turning positive at plus two units and occupancy rebounding to 75.4%. Revenue remained relatively stable around €15,000 monthly despite occupancy swings, with per-sqm rates holding steady near €23.50. Arrears have deteriorated significantly, nearly tripling from €1,253 in June to €3,526 in August across 12 accounts, warranting attention to collections processes.
