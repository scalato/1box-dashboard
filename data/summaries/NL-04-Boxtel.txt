Occupancy at 1BOX Boxtel has deteriorated significantly over the three-month period, declining from 82.1% unit occupancy in May to 73.9% in July, a drop of 8.2 percentage points driven by consistent negative net rentals each month (–3, –5, and –6 units respectively). Revenue has compressed from 15,542 to 14,895 despite a modest uptick in average rate per sqm, reflecting the occupancy losses outweighing pricing gains. Arrears spiked in July to 2,487 outstanding across 12 accounts, nearly double the June level, signaling collection pressure alongside the occupancy decline.
