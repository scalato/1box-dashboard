Occupancy at 1BOX Boxtel improved steadily across the three-month window, with square-meter occupancy rising from 81.7% in August to 84.5% in October, while unit occupancy gained 1.5 percentage points to 76.9%. Rental velocity remained modest at net +1 to +2 units per month, with August recording the strongest move-in activity at 8 units but slowing to 6 vacates in October. Revenue per square meter declined from 23.73 in September to 22.37 in October, a notable 5.8% drop that more than offset the occupancy gains and resulted in lower net revenue in October despite higher unit occupancy. Arrears flagged a concerning jump in October, spiking from 3,119 outstanding to 5,578 across 13 accounts, warranting immediate collection attention.
