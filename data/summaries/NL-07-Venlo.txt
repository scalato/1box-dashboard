Occupancy declined across the three-month window, with unit occupancy falling from 71.7% in June to 69.5% in August despite a stabilizing trend in the most recent month (net -1 rental in August versus -4 and -3 in June and July). Revenue has contracted modestly from €20,525.61 in June to €19,932.82 in August, while average rent per square meter slipped from €18.00 to €17.67 over the same period. Arrears improved significantly from €5,034.09 (24 accounts) in June to €3,261.05 (17 accounts) in July, though they rebounded to €4,601.03 (19 accounts) in August, signaling variable collection momentum.
