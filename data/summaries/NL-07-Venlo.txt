Occupancy at 1BOX Venlo has declined steadily over the three-month period, dropping from 73.5% unit occupancy in May to 69.7% in July—a loss of 7 rented units across the span. Net rentals have turned negative, with the site losing 4 units in June and 3 units in July, as vacates consistently exceeded move-ins. Revenue declined 2.2% from May to July despite relatively flat month-over-month movement in June, driven by both occupancy pressure and a softening average rate per sqm from 18.49 to 17.86. Arrears improved notably in July to 3,261 euros across 17 accounts, down from a peak of 5,034 euros in June, suggesting some collection success offsetting the broader occupancy headwinds.
