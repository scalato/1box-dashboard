Occupancy has steadily improved over the three-month period, rising from 85.7% to 88.0% by unit count and from 85.8% to 91.4% by square meter, with net rentals accelerating to 4 units in October after flat performance in August and September. Revenue has declined modestly from €24,063 to €23,251 month-over-month, driven by a drop in average rent per square meter from €19.65 to €18.87, despite growing occupancy. Arrears have increased materially from €5,344 to €7,067 across the period, though the arrears count fell to 14 in October, suggesting concentration among fewer tenants rather than widespread payment issues.
