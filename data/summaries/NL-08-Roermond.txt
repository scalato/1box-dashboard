Occupancy rebounded in July after declining through May and June, with unit occupancy rising from 83.6% in June to 85.2% in July, driven by a turnaround in net rentals from negative nine units in May to positive two units in July. Revenue improved significantly to €23,465 in July, up 10% from June, with rent-per-sqm jumping to €19.69 from €17.71, suggesting rate increases or mix improvement. However, arrears remain a concern, growing from €2,526 in May to €5,048 in July across 14 accounts, warranting closer collection attention.
