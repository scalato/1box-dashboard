Occupancy at 1BOX Hellevoetsluis has declined steadily over the three-month period, dropping from 86.9% unit occupancy in May to 82.9% in July, a loss of 13 rented units across the span. Rental velocity remains negative, with net losses of 6, 10, and 3 units in May, June, and July respectively, though the July decline has moderated. Revenue dipped in June to €42,194 before recovering slightly to €43,353 in July, while the average rate per square meter held flat around €24.25 before improving to €25.07 in July. Arrears have improved significantly, falling from €4,922 across 40 accounts in May to €3,723 across 18 accounts by July, suggesting better collections despite the occupancy headwinds.
