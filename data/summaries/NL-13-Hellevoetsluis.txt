Occupancy at 1BOX Hellevoetsluis improved steadily from July through September, with unit occupancy rising from 82.9% to 84.7% and square-meter occupancy climbing from 84.0% to 88.7%, driven by net gains of 6 units in August before stabilizing in September. Rental velocity turned positive in August with net +6 rentals, then flattened in September with balanced move-ins and vacates at 13 each. Revenue grew modestly from €43,353 in July to €44,607 in September despite a slight decline in average rent per square meter from €25.07 to €24.66, suggesting the occupancy gains offset pricing pressure. Arrears are a concern, with outstanding debt rising from €3,723 across 18 accounts in July to €4,249 spread across 37 accounts by September, indicating increased collection friction despite overall site strength.
