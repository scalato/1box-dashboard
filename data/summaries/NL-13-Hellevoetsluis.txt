Occupancy by unit count improved from 84.7% in August to 86.0% in October, gaining 4 net units in October after flat performance in September. Square-meter occupancy peaked at 88.7% in September then declined to 86.3% in October, suggesting a shift in mix toward smaller units. Revenue remained relatively stable between 43k–44.6k across the period, with rate-per-sqm holding steady around 24.66–24.92. Arrears outstanding spiked notably to 7,061.93 in October from 4,249.49 in September, though the count of accounts in arrears decreased slightly to 25 from 37.
