Occupancy at 1BOX Hellevoetsluis recovered in August after declining through June and July, rising to 87.1% on a sqm basis (up 3.1 points from July) and 84.7% by unit count, driven by a net gain of 6 units in August versus net losses in the prior two months. Rental velocity improved sharply with move-ins accelerating to 15 in August and vacates dropping to 9, reversing the negative momentum from June and July. Revenue remained relatively flat across the three-month span despite occupancy swings, hovering around €43,200–€43,350 monthly with rates per sqm ranging from €24.24 to €25.07. Arrears represent a growing concern, rising from €2,972 in June to €4,962 in August with account count increasing from 20 to 26 delinquencies, warranting attention.
