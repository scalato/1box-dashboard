Occupancy stabilized in August after declining through June and July, with unit occupancy recovering to 80.4% (up 0.2 points from July's low) and sqm occupancy rising to 84.3%, though both remain below June's levels. Rental velocity turned positive in August with net +1 unit after two consecutive months of losses, driven by a sharp drop in vacates from 24 to 14 units. Revenue climbed steadily across all three months from €68,369 to €72,515, with rent-per-sqm increasing from €25.82 to €27.57, indicating consistent rate growth. Arrears are a concern, rising from €6,320 in June to €10,477 in August (60 accounts flagged), representing a 65% increase in outstanding balance over the period.
