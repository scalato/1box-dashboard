Occupancy has declined across the three-month window, with unit occupancy falling from 80.4% in August to 79.2% in October, while square-meter occupancy dropped 2.7 percentage points from 84.3% to 81.6%. Rental velocity has turned negative, with the site losing 3 units in September and another 3 in October after a flat performance in August, driven by vacates consistently exceeding move-ins. Revenue remained relatively stable at approximately 73.5k per month despite occupancy pressure, supported by a modest rate increase to 27.75 per sqm by October. Arrears have improved slightly to 9,573 outstanding across 64 accounts in October, down from a peak of 11,228 in September.
