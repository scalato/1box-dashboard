1BOX Rijswijk has experienced a significant occupancy decline over the three-month period, with unit occupancy falling from 84.5% in May to 80.2% in July, driven by consecutive months of negative net rentals (net -16 units in June and net -11 units in July). Revenue has remained relatively stable despite the occupancy loss, rising from €69,994 to €70,446, supported by a rate increase from €25.71 to €26.78 per square meter. Arrears have improved markedly, dropping from €10,047 across 97 accounts in May to €7,427 across 51 accounts by July, indicating better collection performance even as the occupancy trend weakens.
