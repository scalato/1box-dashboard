Occupancy by square metre has steadily improved from 83.2% in July to 84.8% in September, while unit occupancy remains relatively flat around 80%, indicating the site is filling larger units. Rental velocity has been inconsistent—July saw a net loss of 11 units, August recovered with a net gain of 1 unit, and September declined again with a net loss of 3 units. Revenue climbed 4.4% over the period from €70,446 to €73,547, driven partly by rate increases (up from €26.78 to €27.83 per sqm), though the rent-per-sqm trajectory has begun to plateau. Arrears are a growing concern, rising sharply from €7,427 in July to €11,228 in September, with delinquent account count up from 51 to 72 over the same span.
