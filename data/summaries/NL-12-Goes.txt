Occupancy peaked in September at 83.7% by square meters before sliding to 80.5% in October, a decline of 3.2 points across the window. Rental velocity weakened significantly in October with a net loss of 3 units after gaining 7 units in September, driven by a sharp drop in move-ins from 15 to just 7. Revenue per square meter improved to 19.53 in October despite the occupancy pullback, though total net revenue growth of nearly 10% since August masks the recent momentum loss. Arrears outstanding remain elevated at 5,893.56, though the count of delinquent accounts improved to 28 in October from 38 in September.
