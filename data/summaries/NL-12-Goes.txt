Unit occupancy has remained flat at 70% across all three months, but square-meter occupancy has declined 3.1 percentage points from May to July, dropping from 80.8% to 77.7%, indicating a shift toward smaller unit rentals. Rental velocity is weak, with net losses of two to three units in May and June followed by breakeven in July; move-ins have slowed significantly to just five in July compared to eight in June. Revenue has remained relatively stable around $30,000 monthly despite occupancy pressure, supported by a modest rate increase from $17.59 to $18.14 per sqm. Arrears are a growing concern, with outstanding balances rising to $5,196.91 and accounts in arrears climbing from 14 to 29 over the period.
