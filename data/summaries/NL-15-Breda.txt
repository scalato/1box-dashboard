Occupancy at 1BOX Breda dipped slightly in July (60.1% unit occupancy) before recovering to 61.7% in August, a net gain of 1.4 percentage points since June. Rental velocity turned negative in July with one net vacancy, but rebounded strongly in August with net +14 units and 38 move-ins, driving revenue up 6.7% to €50,753 and rate-per-sqm up to €13.93. Arrears remain a concern despite the recent improvement, with the outstanding balance and delinquent account count both elevated through August (though outstanding amount declined to €6,454, account count rose to 55 accounts).
