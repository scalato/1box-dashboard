Occupancy at 1BOX Breda has remained essentially flat over the three-month window, hovering between 57.9% and 60.3% on a unit basis, though rental velocity deteriorated sharply in July with net rentals dropping to -1 unit after gains of +17 and +19 in May and June respectively. Revenue has stayed stable around €47.5k monthly despite the slowdown in July, supported by a modest increase in average rate per square meter from €13.02 to €13.46. Arrears have grown concerning, rising from €7.2k in May to €11.8k in July, indicating potential collection pressure despite a reduction in arrears count from 74 to 35 accounts.
