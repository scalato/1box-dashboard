Occupancy at 1BOX Breda has climbed steadily from 60.1% in July to 63.9% in September, driven by consistent positive rental velocity of +14 and +16 net units in August and September respectively. Revenue increased 9.6% over the period from €47,503 to €52,119, though the rate per square meter held relatively flat at €13.46–€13.93. Arrears remain a concern with outstanding balances fluctuating between €6,454 and €11,753 across the three months, though the arrears count peaked at 55 accounts in August before improving slightly to 50 in September.
