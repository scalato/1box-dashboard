Occupancy at 1BOX Breda has risen steadily from 61.7% in August to 64.2% in October, driven by positive net rentals of 14 and 16 units in August and September, though momentum slowed to just 4 net units in October as move-ins dropped to 27. Revenue declined 3.4% from September to October despite a slight uptick in rate-per-sqm to 14.08, indicating the slower leasing pace is offsetting pricing gains. Arrears spiked significantly in September to 11,282.59 before normalizing back down to 5,586.28 in October, with the arrears count declining from 55 to 43 accounts, suggesting improved collections.
