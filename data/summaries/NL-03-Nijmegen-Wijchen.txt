Occupancy by square metre improved steadily from 85.4% in August to 87.8% in October, though unit-level occupancy peaked in September at 86.1% before declining slightly to 85.8% in October. Rental velocity was positive in August and September with net additions of 6 and 7 units respectively, but stalled in October with zero net rentals as vacates (13 units) outpaced move-ins (3 units). Revenue grew 7.9% from August to October despite flat occupancy in recent weeks, driven by rate-per-sqm increases from 20.46 to 20.99. Arrears spiked significantly in September to 5,574.39 across 24 accounts, though improved to 4,254.79 by October while the account count continued climbing to 28.
