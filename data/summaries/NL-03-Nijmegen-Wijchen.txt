Occupancy declined steadily over the three-month span, with square-meter occupancy falling from 87.0% in May to 83.6% in July, and unit occupancy dropping from 83.0% to 82.0%. Rental velocity weakened significantly, moving from a net gain of 6 units in May to flat performance in June and a net loss of 4 units in July, driven by elevated vacates in the final month. Revenue remained relatively stable around 32,300–32,400 despite the occupancy decline, supported by modest rate increases from 20.33 to 20.89 per square meter. Arrears improved substantially in July, falling to 905.82 from a spike of 5,668.19 in June, though the count remained elevated at 19 accounts.
