Occupancy at 1BOX Nijmegen-Wijchen improved steadily over the three-month period, rising 4 percentage points from 82.0% to 86.1% on a unit basis, driven by consistent net rental gains of 6 and 7 units in August and September respectively. Revenue grew 4.3% from July to September despite a slight decline in rate per sqm, reaching €33,824 in September as the added occupied units offset modest pricing pressure. Arrears surged sharply in September to €5,574 across 24 accounts—a concerning jump from €510 in August—suggesting potential collection issues that warrant investigation despite the otherwise positive occupancy momentum.
