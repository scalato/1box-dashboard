Occupancy at 1BOX Nijmegen-Wijchen recovered in August after a dip in July, with square-meter occupancy rising from 83.6% to 85.4% and unit occupancy climbing from 82.0% to 83.7%. Rental velocity turned positive in August with net +6 units after a net loss of 4 units in July, driven by 13 move-ins against only 9 vacates. Revenue remained relatively flat across the three-month period (€32,356 to €32,614), while rent-per-sqm declined slightly from €20.89 to €20.46. Arrears improved significantly, dropping from €5,668 in June to just €510 in August, indicating better collection performance despite an uptick in arrears account count.
